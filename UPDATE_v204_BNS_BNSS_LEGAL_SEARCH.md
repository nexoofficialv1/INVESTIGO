# INVESTIGO v204 — BNS / BNSS Offline Legal Search

## Scope
- Offline searchable BNS + BNSS section/subsection index.
- Keyword, section number, and old IPC/CrPC mapping search.
- Case-section shortcut (e.g. `281/125(b)/324(4) BNS`).
- Verified official core text for high-use investigation sections.
- Bengali helper explanation and on-device Bengali translation.
- Clear source-tier badge: official verified vs comparison/index.
- Draft-bill mismatch guard: BNS 281 is final enacted `Rash driving or riding on a public way`, not the earlier Bill numbering.

## Data provenance
1. Authoritative/verified core statutory wording: Ministry of Home Affairs Gazette PDFs / India Code, Act 45 and Act 46 of 2023.
2. Search index and IPC/CrPC correspondence: user-supplied comparison PDFs.
3. Bengali generated translation is explicitly labelled helper/non-official.

## Important limitation
v204 bundles the complete comparison-derived searchable section/subsection index, but only a curated core set carries verified official statutory text/extracts. Entries without a verified bundle are visibly labelled `Comparison/index source`; the app must not present those notes as the authoritative bare Act text.
